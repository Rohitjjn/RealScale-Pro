RealScale-Pro Privacy Policy

Every photo, scan, screenshot, illustration, and animated GIF you open is processed 100% locally on your Android device. The project operates no backend servers, requires no user account, collects zero personal information, and bundles zero analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Published by Rohit (@Rohitjjn) · Effective November 16, 2026

How Images and Local Data Are Handled

On-Device Image Processing

Input images selected via the Android Photo Picker, Storage Access Framework, camera capture, or the system Share Sheet are decoded into the app's private cache directory, processed by local native binaries on your phone's GPU or CPU, and written to Pictures/RealScale-Pro/ or your chosen folder. No image pixel or EXIF field ever leaves your handset.

Local Preferences and Creation History

Theme preference, default processing configuration, local output history (Room database creations.db), and downloaded model weights live exclusively inside your phone's sandboxed storage. Clearing app storage, tapping Clear history or Clear cache in Settings, or uninstalling removes this local data immediately. Optional EXIF preservation copies camera date, exposure, and orientation tags directly between your local input and local output file and can be toggled off at any time.

Network Requests and Android Permissions

When the Network Is Used

The application makes outbound HTTPS requests only when you choose to download the initial Starter Pack or an optional neural network from the Model Hub. Files are retrieved over TLS from the public repository huggingface.co/tumuyan2/realsr-models (governed by Hugging Face's standard HTTPS access policy) and verified locally against hardcoded SHA-256 digests before installation. Once your preferred models are installed, you can disable network access or work in airplane mode indefinitely.

Declared Manifest Permissions

  • INTERNET: Used solely for user-initiated HTTPS downloads of model weights from Hugging Face.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC & WAKE_LOCK: Keeps active GPU or batch jobs running reliably while the screen is locked or another app is open.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Displays live tile progress and completion notifications (optional).
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Legacy Storage: Reads selected source files and saves finished outputs to your gallery.
