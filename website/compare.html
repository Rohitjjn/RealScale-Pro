RealScale-Pro vs. Remini, Upscayl & RealSR-NCNN-Android

Choosing an AI image upscaler comes down to three trade-offs: where your photos are processed (on your phone vs. a remote server), which models you can run (general restoration, face hallucination, or anime line-art), and what hardware you have (an Android phone vs. a desktop GPU). Here is an honest breakdown including where competing tools win.

When Should You Pick Each Tool?

Pick RealScale-Pro when...

You want to upscale photos, scans, screenshots, anime, or GIFs directly on an Android phone with complete offline privacy, zero subscriptions, a small 20.5 MB install, quick Share/Open-with access, and curated models like Remacri, UltraSharp, Real-ESRGAN, Waifu2x, and SRMD.

Pick Desktop Upscayl or chaiNNer when...

You are batch-upscaling hundreds of 24-megapixel RAW exports on a desktop NVIDIA, AMD, or Apple Silicon GPU where 150 W+ graphics cards finish 10–30× faster than a mobile SoC.

Pick Upstream RealSR-NCNN-Android when...

You specifically want a single offline 475 MB APK with every model pre-installed out of the box, or you routinely type raw ./realsr-ncnn and ./magick shell pipelines inside an in-app terminal.

Pick a Cloud Face Enhancer when...

You need heavy generative face hallucination (GFPGAN / CodeFormer / diffusion) on severely motion-blurred portraits and you are comfortable uploading the photo and paying for server compute.
