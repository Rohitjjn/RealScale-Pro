About RealScale-Pro

RealScale-Pro exists because enlarging a family portrait, a scanned document, or a favorite illustration on Android should not require uploading personal images to a remote server, paying a weekly subscription, or juggling command-line GPU flags.

Maintained by Rohit (@Rohitjjn) · Last updated November 16, 2026

Open-Source Foundations & Attribution

RealScale-Pro builds a complete Kotlin + Jetpack Compose studio experience, automated device tiering, sequential thermal-guarded execution, and on-demand SHA-256 verified model delivery on top of proven open-source mobile inference engines:

  • tumuyan/RealSR-NCNN-Android (MIT) — upstream Android native binary integration across Real-ESRGAN, RealSR, Waifu2x, SRMD, MNN, Anime4KCPP, ImageMagick, and Lanczos resizing.
  • Tencent ncnn (BSD-3-Clause) & nihui/realsr-ncnn-vulkan (MIT) — cross-vendor Vulkan compute inference for mobile GPUs.
  • Alibaba MNN (Apache-2.0) — mobile inference engine powering RealESRGAN Anime 6B.
  • Real-ESRGAN, RealSR, Waifu2x, SRMD, Anime4KCPP, and community model authors detailed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
