How to Use RealScale-Pro

How Do You Upscale a Photo Offline on Android?

RealScale-Pro replaces command-line tile calculations with an automatic deterministic planner (PipelinePlanner) that adapts to 4 GB budget phones and 16 GB flagships alike.

1. Hardware Check & Starter Pack

On first launch, RealScale-Pro profiles your RAM, CPU cores, and Vulkan GPU into Light, Standard, or Powerful and downloads the 7.9 MB Starter Pack with HTTP Range pause/resume.

2. Import, Share, or Widget

Pick images via the Android system Gallery picker, Files (SD cards & cloud drives), Camera, the system-wide Share / Open-with Quick Sheet, or the 2×/3×/5× home-screen widget.

3. Upscale Safely & Compare

Watch live tiles resolve with rolling ETA, continue in the background while ThermalGuard keeps your phone cool, compare on the split slider, and save with EXIF metadata intact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About RealScale-Pro

Does RealScale-Pro upload photos to the cloud?

No. RealScale-Pro processes every image locally on your Android phone's Vulkan GPU, OpenCL backend, or CPU. Your images, crops, and EXIF metadata never leave your device, and there are zero user accounts, ads, or analytics SDKs. Network access is used solely to download AI model weights on demand from Hugging Face.

Why is the RealScale-Pro APK only 20.5 MB?

Traditional Android NCNN apps bundle roughly 440 MB of neural network weights inside a 475 MB APK. RealScale-Pro removes bundled weights, solid-compresses all 8 native arm64 engines into a 15.6 MiB binaries.tar.xz archive unpacked once on first run, and lets you download only the models you use — starting with a 7.9 MB Starter Pack verified by SHA-256 checksums.

Which AI models does RealScale-Pro support on Android?

RealScale-Pro includes 14 SHA-256 verified models in its Model Manager: Real-ESRGAN General v3, Waifu2x Anime, Waifu2x Photo, Waifu2x CUnet, SRMD (2×/3×/4×), RealSR DF2K, RealSR DF2K JPEG, Remacri x4, UltraMix Balanced x4, UltraSharp x4 (fp16), Nomos8kSC x4, AnimeSharp Lite x4, AnimeSharp x4, and RealESRGAN Anime 6B (MNN), plus bundled Anime4K shaders. Browse full specs on the Models page.

What Android devices can run RealScale-Pro?

Any 64-bit ARM (arm64-v8a) phone or tablet running Android 7.0 (API 24) or later with 4 GB+ RAM. A Vulkan 1.1+ GPU (Adreno, Mali, Xclipse, or Tensor) provides the fastest processing; a CPU fallback mode is available in Settings and Advanced config for older drivers.

How do 3× and 5× scaling work with 2× and 4× AI models?

RealScale-Pro runs each neural network at a native scale its weights support (4× for Real-ESRGAN/Remacri/UltraSharp, 2× for Waifu2x, or native 2×/3×/4× for SRMD), measures the rendered image, and runs one high-precision avir-lancir Lanczos pass to match your target resolution to the pixel.

Is RealScale-Pro completely free and open source?

Yes. RealScale-Pro costs $0, includes zero ads, watermarks, or subscriptions, and is released under the MIT License. Inspect, build, fork, or contribute on GitHub.
